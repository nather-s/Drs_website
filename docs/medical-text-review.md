## Pcos  (/treatments/pcos/)

- Clinic name changed from "Harleys Cosmetic & Women Clinic" to the confirmed "Harleys Women's Clinic, Andheri West" (mentioned once in the intro).
- Cysts: old text said the cysts "contain immature eggs called follicles". Corrected: the sacs are themselves follicles, each holding an immature egg.
- Hormones: old text said oestrogen, progesterone and FSH "drop" in PCOS. Corrected: oestrogen is usually not low; testosterone, insulin and LH are often raised (LH high relative to FSH), and progesterone stays low because ovulation is irregular. Please check.
- Ovulation medicines: old text said "oral oestrogen medication, a breast cancer drug". Corrected: clomiphene is an anti-oestrogen tablet, and the breast cancer drug is letrozole. The diabetes drug is named generically (metformin) and the injections as gonadotrophins.
- Laparoscopic ovarian drilling: old text described an abdominal incision, "removal of tissues" and destroying "large ovarian cysts". Corrected to keyhole surgery that makes a few tiny punctures in the ovary surface with laser or heat to reduce androgen-producing tissue; it does not remove cysts. Also dropped "If PCOS gets severe, surgery may be needed": surgery is for fertility, not severity.
- Ultrasound: old text described only an internal (vaginal) scan. Added that the scan is done at the clinic and is done over the tummy, not internally, for patients who have never been sexually active.
- Merged in from the existing page (not in old text): the 2023 guideline point that ultrasound is not used to diagnose PCOS within 8 years of the first period; AMH as an alternative to ultrasound in adults; "other causes ruled out" in diagnosis; thyroid tests; 5–10% weight loss; the warning that anti-androgens need reliable contraception; endometrial thickening with infrequent periods.
- "Low-calorie diet" softened to "healthy, balanced diet". "Progestin" written as "progestogen" (British usage).
- FAQ "Can pregnancy cure PCOS?": old wording suggested getting pregnant can relieve symptoms. Reworded so it does not read as advice to get pregnant to treat PCOS.
- FAQ "How do healthcare providers diagnose PCOS?": "cysts" changed to "follicles", and "other causes ruled out" added.
- FAQ on age: "obese people" changed to "overweight". "Any woman or individuals assigned female at birth" simplified to "any time after puberty".
- Old text said treatment makes patients "eventually less aware" of the condition. Reworded as "controls symptoms rather than curing it; your plan can change over time".
- "PCOS is not a serious health issue only if it's early diagnosed" reworded. Heart attack and coronary artery disease merged as "heart disease such as heart attack".
- "PCOS and Associated Concerns" kept as plain points (the old site linked to separate pages; weight and mental health have no page on the new site). The garbled fertility line ("primary cause of more androgen production") was rewritten.
